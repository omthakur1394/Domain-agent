ShopEase Category-Specific Policy Knowledge 
Base (KB)  
Document Owner: Category Management & Customer Experience Team  
Last Updated: June 2026  
Classification: Internal Confidential  
1. Electronics & Mobile Devices  
1.1 Scope & Return Window  
Covers smartphones, laptops, tablets, audio devices, and smart wearables. The standard 
return/replacement window is 7 days from delivery.  
1.2 Policy Guidelines: Warranty, Repair, and Replacement  
● Dead on Arrival (DOA) / Out of Box Failure: If a device is defective within the 7-day window, 
ShopEase provides a direct Replacement Only (no refunds unless the item is out of stock).  
● Post 7-Day Defects (Brand Warranty): Beyond 7 days, ShopEase is no longer liable for 
replacements. Customers must claim the manufacturer's warranty at an authorized brand service 
center.  
● Repairs: ShopEase does not operate in-house repair services for electronics.  
1.3 Step-by-Step Resolution (Defect Claim within 7 Days)  
1. Initial Triage: Ask the customer to perform basic troubleshooting (e.g., hard reset, checking 
different charging cables).  
2. Brand Certificate Requirement (Mobiles/Laptops): For high-value electronics, the customer must 
visit a brand service center and obtain a "DOA Certificate" or "Defect Confirmation Slip" before 
ShopEase approves the replacement.  
3. Approve Replacement: Once the certificate is uploaded to the chat/ticket, initiate a reverse pickup.  
4. Dispatch Replacement: The replacement unit is dispatched only after the defective unit reaches 
the central hub and passes the IMEI/Serial Number verification check.  
2. Large Appliances (TVs, ACs, Refrigerators, Washing 
Machines)  
2.1 Scope & Delivery Operations  
Large appliances require specialized handling. Deliveries are typically classified as "Open Box Deliveries" 
(OBD) to prevent transit damage claims.  2.2 Policy Guidelines  
● Installation & Demo: Delivery and installation are two separate processes. Installation/Demo is 
scheduled within 48 business hours of successful delivery by brand-authorized technicians.  
● Disposal of Old Products (Exchange Program): If a customer purchased via an "Exchange Offer," 
the delivery executive will evaluate and pick up the old appliance at the exact time the new 
appliance is delivered.  
● Open Box Delivery (OBD): Customers must allow the delivery agent to open the packaging at the 
doorstep to check for physical damage.  
2.3 Step-by-Step Resolution  
● Scenario A: Damage Found During Delivery: The customer must refuse the delivery. The agent 
updates the status to "Transit Damage," and an automatic replacement is triggered.  
● Scenario B: Damage Found During Installation: If the installation technician opens a sealed box 
and finds internal damage, ask the customer to upload a photo of the technician's report. Initiate 
an immediate replacement.  
● Scenario C: Delayed Installation: If 48 hours have passed since delivery, escalate to the Category 
Logistics > Large Appliances queue to expedite the brand technician. Offer ₹200 ShopEase Wallet 
credit for the delay.  
3. Grocery & FMCG (Fast-Moving Consumer Goods)  
3.1 Scope & Nature of Goods  
Covers fresh produce, dairy, meat, and packaged food items (FMCG).  
3.2 Policy Guidelines  
● Freshness Guarantee: Perishable goods (fruits, vegetables, dairy, meat) have a No Return Policy. If 
quality is compromised, ShopEase provides a Direct Refund.  
● Expiry Complaints: Packaged goods must have at least 30 days of shelf life remaining at the time of 
delivery (unless explicitly sold under a "Clearance/Short Expiry" tag).  
3.3 Step-by-Step Resolution  
1. Perishable Quality Issue (Reported within 24 hours):  
○ Request a photograph of the spoiled/damaged item.  
○ Do not initiate a reverse pickup.  
○ Process a partial or full refund directly to the source account based on the quantity damaged.  
2. Expired/Near-Expiry Product Received:  
○ Request a photo clearly showing the manufacturing date (MFG) and expiry date/best before 
date on the packaging.  
○ If the item was expired on delivery or has less than 30 days remaining, process a 100% refund 
immediately. No return pickup required for food safety reasons.  4. Fashion & Apparel  
4.1 Scope & Return Window  
Covers clothing, footwear, and accessories. The standard return/exchange window is 14 days from 
delivery.  
4.2 Policy Guidelines  
● Fit Issues & Size Guides: ShopEase provides standardized brand size charts, but sizing varies. Fit 
issues are the #1 reason for returns and are covered under the "No Questions Asked" 14-day policy.  
● Condition of Return: Items must be unworn, unwashed, and have all original brand tags securely 
attached.  
● Alterations: ShopEase does not provide custom tailoring or alteration services.  
● Non-Returnable Items: For hygiene reasons, innerwear, lingerie, swimwear, and socks cannot be 
returned or exchanged.  
4.3 Step-by-Step Resolution (Fit Issue)  
1. Confirm Request: Check if the item is eligible (not innerwear, within 14 days).  
2. Offer Exchange First: Ask if the customer prefers a different size. If the desired size is in stock, 
process a direct Exchange Request.  
3. Refund Alternative: If out of stock, or if the customer prefers a refund, initiate a Return.  
4. Remind of Tags: Clearly state to the customer: "Please ensure all original tags are attached, as the 
pickup agent will check this at your doorstep."  
5. Furniture & Home Decor  
5.1 Scope & Operations  
Covers beds, sofas, wardrobes, and dining tables.  
5.2 Policy Guidelines  
● Assembly Services: ShopEase provides free assembly for products explicitly marked "Assembly 
Provided." This is done by a specialized carpentry partner within 72 hours of delivery.  
● Damage on Delivery: Furniture is highly susceptible to transit damage. Issues must be reported 
within 3 days.  
● Part Replacement: To save logistics costs, ShopEase prioritizes shipping missing/damaged 
components (e.g., a broken table leg, missing screws) rather than replacing the entire bulky unit.  
5.3 Step-by-Step Resolution (Damage / Missing Parts)  
1. Identify the Issue: Ask the customer to use the instruction manual to identify the exact part 
number/letter that is damaged or missing.  
2. Photo Evidence: Request photos of the damaged part and the outer packaging.  
3. Resolution Path:  ○ If a minor part is damaged: Raise a request with the seller to courier the specific replacement 
part. Inform the customer of the tracking details.  
○ If the main structural frame is damaged: Initiate a full reverse pickup and full 
replacement/refund.  
6. Books & Media  
6.1 Scope & Common Issues  
Covers physical books, academic textbooks, and graphic novels. Common issues include damaged 
spines, torn pages, or incorrect editions.  
6.2 Policy Guidelines  
● Edition Mismatch: Academic books are highly sensitive to edition changes. If a customer ordered 
the 10th edition but received the 9th edition, this is classified as an "Item Not As Described" (INAD) 
defect.  
● Counterfeit Books (Pirated Copies): ShopEase heavily penalizes sellers shipping 
photocopied/pirated books disguised as originals.  
6.3 Step-by-Step Resolution (Edition Mismatch)  
1. Verify Listing vs. Received Item: Request a photo of the book cover and the copyright page 
(showing the edition/year). Compare this to the ShopEase product listing.  
2. Initiate Replacement/Refund: Create a return request. Offer the customer a refund or a 
replacement (ensure a different seller fulfills the replacement to avoid a repeat error).  
3. Seller Strike: Tag the ticket to backend operations to penalize the seller for catalog mismatch.  
7. Pharmacy, Health & Wellness  
7.1 Scope & Regulatory Compliance  
Covers over-the-counter (OTC) medicines, prescription drugs (Rx), supplements, and medical devices. 
This category has strict legal regulations.  
7.2 Policy Guidelines  
● Prescription (Rx) Requirements: Drugs marked as "Rx Required" cannot be dispatched until a valid 
prescription from a registered medical practitioner is uploaded by the customer and verified by 
ShopEase's in-house pharmacists.  
● Cold Chain Products: Items requiring temperature control (e.g., insulin, certain vaccines) are 
shipped with ice packs in insulated boxes.  
● Returns Policy: Medicines are strictly non-returnable once delivered, due to safety and tampering 
concerns. Refunds are only issued for incorrect/damaged deliveries.  
● Expiry Policy: Medicines must have a minimum of 6 months before the expiry date at the time of 
delivery.  7.3 Step-by-Step Resolution  
● Scenario A: Prescription Rejected: If the customer asks why their order is stalled, explain that the 
in-house pharmacist rejected the prescription (e.g., too old, illegible, missing doctor's signature). 
Ask them to upload a fresh, valid prescription via the App.  
● Scenario B: Expired Medicine Delivered: Request a photo of the batch number and expiry date. If 
valid, issue an immediate 100% refund. Direct the customer to safely dispose of the medicine; do 
not arrange a reverse pickup.  
● Scenario C: Cold Chain Failure: If the customer reports that an insulin package arrived warm and 
the ice packs were completely melted, issue an immediate replacement or refund. Raise an SLA 
breach ticket against the specialized 3PL courier.

ShopEase Legal, Escalations & Compliance 
Knowledge Base (KB)  
Document Owner: Legal, Compliance & Grievance Redressal Team  
Last Updated: June 2026  
Classification: Internal Confidential  
1. Escalation Matrix and Response SLAs  
ShopEase follows a strict three-tier escalation matrix to ensure timely resolution of customer issues in 
accordance with Indian e-commerce regulations.  
1.1 Support Levels & SLAs  
● Tier 1 (Frontline Support): Handles general queries (Where is my order, basic returns).  
○ First Response SLA: 2 hours.  
○ Resolution SLA: 24-48 business hours.  
● Tier 2 (Escalation Desk): Handles breached SLAs, deadlocked buyer-seller disputes, and 
compensation requests.  
○ First Response SLA: 12 hours.  
○ Resolution SLA: 72 business hours.  
● Tier 3 (Grievance Redressal Mechanism): Handles legal threats, privacy breaches, consumer court 
notices, and unresolved Tier 2 tickets.  
○ Acknowledgment SLA: 48 hours (Mandated by law).  
○ Resolution SLA: Maximum 30 days from receipt of the complaint.  
2. Formal Complaint & Grievance Redressal Process  
2.1 How Customers Can File a Formal Complaint  
If a customer is dissatisfied with Tier 1 and Tier 2 resolutions, they have the right to escalate the issue 
formally.  
1. Grievance Portal: Customers must log in and navigate to My Account > Help Center > Escalate to 
Grievance Officer.  
2. Email Initiation: Alternatively, customers can email the dedicated grievance desk directly with their 
Ticket ID.  
2.2 Grievance Officer Details (Statutory Requirement)  
Under the Consumer Protection (E-Commerce) Rules, 2020, ShopEase has appointed a resident 
Grievance Officer.

1. How Third-Party Sellers Work on ShopEase  
1.1 The Marketplace Model  
ShopEase operates as an online marketplace. While some products are sold directly by ShopEase Retail, 
the vast majority are sold by independent, third-party businesses. ShopEase acts as the facilitator 
(intermediary) connecting buyers with these sellers, providing the platform, payment gateway, and often 
the logistics.  
1.2 Fulfillment Models  
● Fulfilled by ShopEase (FBE): The seller stores their inventory in ShopEase warehouses.  
ShopEase handles picking, packing, shipping, and customer service for returns.  
● Seller-Fulfilled (SF): The seller keeps the inventory at their own premises. When an order is placed, 
the seller packs the item, and a ShopEase logistics partner (or a third-party courier) picks it up for 
delivery.  
1.3 Legal Disclaimer (Agent Scripting)  
Agents must clarify to customers: "ShopEase provides the platform for independent sellers. The contract 
of sale is strictly between you and the seller. However, ShopEase protects your purchase under the 
ShopEase Buyer Protection Guarantee."  
2. Seller Ratings and Accountability  
2.1 Seller Rating System  
Sellers are rated on a 5-star scale based on customer feedback, product quality, shipping speed (for SF 
orders), and cancellation rates.  
2.2 Accountability & Penalties  
● Performance Metrics: Sellers must maintain a minimum rating of 3.5 stars.  
● High Returns: If a seller's Return Rate exceeds 15% in a specific category, their listings will be 
temporarily suppressed from search results.  
● Auto-Delisting: Sellers whose ratings drop below 2.5 stars for two consecutive months are 
automatically suspended from the platform.  2.3 Agent Action  
When a customer complains about a poor experience that doesn't violate a specific policy, agents should 
encourage the customer to leave a factual Seller Review, as this directly impacts the seller's visibility and 
platform privileges.  
3. Reporting a Fraudulent Seller  
3.1 Scenario Definition  
A customer alleges that a seller is intentionally defrauding buyers (e.g., sending empty boxes, sending 
bricks/stones instead of electronics, collecting data outside the platform).  
3.2 Policy Guidelines  
● Immediate Escalation: Fraud reports bypass standard Tier 1 troubleshooting and go directly to the 
Trust & Safety (T&S) team.  
● SLA for Action: T&S must review the seller's account within 24 business hours.  
3.3 Step-by-Step Resolution  
1. Gather Evidence: Request unboxing videos, photos of the package weight/shipping label, and the 
contents received.  
2. Log the Incident: Create a high-priority ticket under the category Marketplace Trust > Fraudulent 
Seller.  
3. Freeze Payouts: Initiate an automated hold on the seller's pending payouts via the OMS (Order 
Management System) to protect buyer funds.  
4. Resolution to Buyer: If evidence heavily supports the buyer (or if multiple buyers report the same 
seller simultaneously), issue an immediate 100% refund without waiting for a reverse pickup.  
5. Seller Ban: T&S will permanently ban the seller and blacklist their GSTIN and bank details.  
4. Counterfeit Product Complaints  
4.1 Scenario Definition  
A customer claims the product received is a knock-off, replica, or fake version of a branded item.  
4.2 Policy Guidelines  
ShopEase maintains a "Zero Tolerance" policy for counterfeit goods. Sellers found violating this are 
subject to immediate account termination and potential legal action.  
4.3 Step-by-Step Resolution

missing holograms, spelling errors on packaging, poor build quality).

An order is cancelled by the seller before dispatch due to being Out of Stock (OOS), a pricing error, or

5.3 Common FAQs 
Q: I received a damaged product. What should I do? 
A: Do not use the product. Take photos of the damaged packaging and product. Raise a 
return request from 'My Orders' within 48 hours of delivery, select 'Damaged Product' as 
the reason, and upload the photos. A replacement or full refund will be arranged within 
3-5 business days. 
Q: I received the wrong item. What should I do? A: Go to 'My Orders', select the order, click 'Return Item', and choose 'Wrong Item 
Received'. Upload photos of the item and the packaging label. We will arrange a pickup 
and send the correct item at no additional charge, or issue a full refund. 
Q: My order is showing delivered but I have not received it. 
A: First, check with neighbours or your building security in case the package was left 
with them. If still not found, report it within 48 hours of the delivery status update by 
contacting support. We will raise an investigation with our delivery partner and resolve it 
within 3-5 business days. 
Q: How long does it take to get my refund? 
A: Refund timelines depend on your payment method. UPI/Wallet refunds are 
processed within 24-48 hours. Credit/Debit cards take 5-7 business days. Net Banking 
takes 3-5 business days. COD refunds go to your ShopEase Wallet instantly or to your 
bank account in 7-10 business days. 
Q: Can I change my delivery address after placing the order? 
A: Address changes are only possible within 1 hour of placing the order and before the 
order is packed. Go to 'My Orders', select the order, and click 'Modify Order' to update 
the address. Changes are restricted to the same city. 
Q: What if I am not available at home during delivery? 
A: Our delivery partner will attempt delivery 3 times. You will receive a call before each 
attempt. You can also reschedule delivery through the tracking link sent to you, or 
authorize someone at the delivery address to receive the package on your behalf. 
Q: Is Cash on Delivery available for my PIN code? 
A: COD availability depends on your PIN code and order value (maximum Rs. 10,000). 
You can check COD availability by entering your PIN code on the product page or 
during checkout. COD is not available for certain high-value electronics and perishable 
items. 
Q: How do I track my order? 
A: You can track your order in 'My Orders' section using your Order ID. You will also 
receive SMS and email updates at each delivery milestone. If your order has been 
dispatched, a tracking number for the courier partner is provided in the shipment 
notification email.
